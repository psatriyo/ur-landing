Getting started

How do I get started?
  1. Download UangRumah from the App Store.
  2. Open the app and follow the short introduction. Your first book is created for you.
  3. Set a monthly or weekly budget in Settings → Budget.
  4. Check your categories and cost centers (payment methods) in Settings.
  5. Tap + on the Manage tab to add your first expense.
  6. Optional: turn on Expense Nudge in Settings → Preferences for a daily reminder.
What is a book?

A book is a separate ledger with its own expenses, budget, categories, cost centers and vendors. Most people use one book for the household. Add a second for a side business or a big project. You can have up to 2 books and switch between them from the book icon at the top of the screen.

Is UangRumah free?

Yes. All features are free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later. There's no Android version at the moment.

Expenses & budgets

How do I add an expense?

Go to the Manage tab and tap +. Enter a name and amount. Everything else is optional: vendor, category, cost center, date and notes. Tap Add Expense to save.

To edit or delete, tap an expense or swipe left on it.

What is Batch Entry Mode?

Batch Entry Mode lets you add several expenses without closing the form. After each save the form clears and keeps the date you picked, so entering a stack of receipts is quick. Turn it on in the Add Expense form.

How do recurring expenses work?

When adding an expense, turn on Recurring Expense and choose how often it repeats: daily, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly or yearly. You can add an end date. When a payment is due, UangRumah adds it for you the next time you open the app.

How do budget alerts work?

Set a budget in Settings → Budget and choose when to be alerted: at 50%, 75%, 90% or 100%. UangRumah compares what you've spent this month with what your budget allows up to today, and sends a notification when you reach that level.

Make sure notifications for UangRumah are allowed in iOS Settings.

What does the daily figure on the Dashboard mean?

It's the budget you have left divided by the days left in the month (or week). If you spend less than this each day, you'll finish within budget. The status shows On Track, Watch Out or Over Budget by comparing how much you've spent with how much of the month has passed.

What are cost centers?

Cost centers are the ways you pay: cash, a bank account, a credit card or an e-wallet. Pick one when you add an expense, and the Dashboard shows your spending by payment method. Each book can have up to 3 cost centers. Manage them in Settings → Cost Centers.

What is Expense Nudge?

A friendly reminder to log your spending. Choose the time and the days (every day, weekdays or days you pick) in Settings → Preferences → Expense Nudge.

Can I see what changed in an expense?

Yes. UangRumah keeps an edit history for every expense. Open the expense to see earlier versions, with changes highlighted.

Books & your data

How many books can I create?

Up to 2 books. Book names can be up to 9 characters long so they fit neatly at the top of the screen. Manage books in Settings → Books.

How do I export my expenses?

On the Manage tab, tap the export icon, or go to Settings → Create Report. Choose PDF for a formatted report or CSV for spreadsheets, pick a date range (this week, this month, this year or custom), then save or share the file.

How do I back up and restore?

Go to Settings → Backup & Restore. Export Backup saves a backup file of the current book that you can keep in Files or share. Import Backup restores a backup file as a new book, so your existing data is never overwritten.

How do I change the currency or language?

Go to Settings → Preferences. You can choose English, Bahasa Indonesia or Bahasa Melayu, and any of more than 150 currencies. The currency applies across the whole app. You can also choose light, dark or system appearance there.

How do I delete a book?

Go to Settings → Books and swipe left on the book. You'll be asked to confirm twice, because deleting a book permanently removes all of its expenses, budgets, categories and cost centers, on every device that syncs with it. Export a backup first if you might need the data later.

Sync & privacy

How does iCloud sync work?

If you're signed in to iCloud, UangRumah syncs automatically between your iPhone and iPad through your private iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. There's no switch to turn on inside the app. Use the same Apple ID on each device. You can check the sync status in Settings → iCloud Sync.

App preferences such as language, currency and reminders are saved on each device and don't sync.

Can I use UangRumah without iCloud?

Yes. If you're not signed in to iCloud, or you turn off iCloud for UangRumah in iOS Settings, your data stays only on that device. In that case, export a backup regularly from Settings → Backup & Restore, because deleting the app would delete your data.

Can my family see my expenses?

Only people who use your Apple ID on a device can see your synced data. Sharing a book between different Apple IDs isn't supported yet. To share numbers with someone, send them a PDF report or a CSV export.

Can the UangRumah team see my data?

No. We don't run servers, there's no account, and the app contains no analytics or advertising code. Your data stays on your devices and in your private iCloud account, which we can't access. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

What is Hide Numbers?

Hide Numbers blurs amounts on your screen, which is useful in public. Tap the eye icon on the Dashboard to hide or show amounts, or set the default in Settings → Preferences.

Troubleshooting

My data isn't syncing between devices
  1. Check that each device is signed in to the same Apple ID.
  2. In iOS Settings, tap your name → iCloud and make sure UangRumah is allowed to use iCloud.
  3. Make sure both devices are online, and that your iCloud storage isn't full.
  4. Open UangRumah on both devices and wait a few minutes. The first sync after installing can take longer.
  5. Check Settings → iCloud Sync in UangRumah for the current status.
  6. Still stuck? Restart both devices, then contact us.
I see "no book" after installing on a new device

When you install UangRumah on another device, your books download from iCloud in the background. This can take a few minutes, especially with a lot of data.

  1. Stay connected to the internet and keep the app open for a few minutes.
  2. Check that you're signed in to the same Apple ID as your other device.
  3. If you don't use iCloud, create a new book, or restore one from a backup file.
I'm not getting notifications
  1. Open iOS Settings → Notifications → UangRumah and turn on Allow Notifications.
  2. In UangRumah, check Settings → Preferences to make sure notifications and Expense Nudge are on.
  3. Budget alerts need a budget: set one in Settings → Budget.
  4. Check that a Focus mode isn't silencing UangRumah.
The Dashboard numbers look wrong
  1. Check you're looking at the right book (book icon at the top).
  2. Check the date on recent expenses, since only expenses in the current month or week count.
  3. Check your week start day (Sunday or Monday) in the budget settings.
  4. If you just added expenses on another device, give iCloud a moment to sync.
The app crashes or won't open
  1. Update UangRumah to the latest version from the App Store.
  2. Update iOS or iPadOS, then restart your device.
  3. Make sure your device has some free storage.
  4. Email us with your device model, iOS version and what happened just before the crash.

Before reinstalling: if you don't use iCloud, deleting the app also deletes your data. Export a backup first if you can.

System requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later. iCloud is optional and is used for sync between your devices.
