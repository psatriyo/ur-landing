Privacy Policy

The short version: your financial data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. We don't collect it, see it or sell it.

Summary

We collect nothing

No account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising in the app.

Stored on your device

Your records are saved on your iPhone or iPad and work offline.

Synced through your iCloud

When you're signed in to iCloud, data syncs through your private iCloud account. We can't access it.

No ads, no selling

Your financial information is never shown to advertisers, shared or sold.

Who we are

UangRumah is an independent app developed by Prayudi Satriyo in Jakarta, Indonesia ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the UangRumah app for iPhone and iPad and the website uangrumah.com handle information. If you have questions, email support@uangrumah.com.

Information we collect

We don't collect personal information. UangRumah has no sign-up, we don't operate any servers that receive your data, and the app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics. This is controlled by Apple and you can turn it off at any time.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and use it only to reply to you.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Expenses: name, amount, vendor, date, category, cost center and notes
  • Edit history of expenses
  • Books, budgets and alert thresholds
  • Categories, cost centers and vendors you create
  • Recurring expense settings
  • App preferences such as language, currency, appearance, reminders and Hide Numbers

This data is protected by iOS security, including your device passcode.

iCloud sync

UangRumah uses Apple's CloudKit to keep your books in sync across devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Sync happens automatically when you're signed in to iCloud and iCloud is allowed for UangRumah. There is no separate switch inside the app.

  • Your data is stored in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple ID can access. We have no access to it.
  • Apple encrypts iCloud data in transit and at rest. iCloud is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • To stop syncing, open iOS Settings, tap your name → iCloud, and turn off iCloud for UangRumah. Your data then stays only on that device.
  • App preferences (language, currency, appearance, reminders) stay on each device and are not synced.

Notifications

Budget alerts, Expense Nudge reminders and the monthly summary are scheduled locally on your device. Apple may send the app silent background notifications that tell it your iCloud data has changed. These contain no personal content.

Exports and backups

PDF reports, CSV exports and backup files are created on your device and saved or shared wherever you choose (for example the Files app, email or messaging apps). Once you share a file, its privacy depends on where you put it.

What we never do

  • Ask you to create an account or collect your name, email or phone number in the app
  • Track your location or your behaviour across apps and websites
  • Use analytics services such as Google Analytics or Firebase
  • Include advertising SDKs or show ads
  • Sell, rent or share your data with third parties
  • Send your financial data to our own servers (we don't have any)

Your choices and rights

You have full control over your data, directly in the app:

  • Access and correct: view and edit every record at any time.
  • Export: download your data as PDF, CSV or a backup file.
  • Delete: delete individual expenses or a whole book. Deleting the app removes local data. To remove synced data, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Because we don't hold your data, we can't access, correct or delete it for you, but we're happy to explain how.

Children

UangRumah is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

uangrumah.com is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. Your language and theme choices are saved in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how your data is handled, we'll also mention it in the app or the App Store release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@uangrumah.com. We aim to reply within 7 business days.
