Why UangRumah

Money tracking that fits real life

Made for households first, with room for your side business too. UangRumah means "house money" in Indonesian.

Fast enough to keep up

Add an expense in a few taps. Vendors auto-complete, recurring bills add themselves, and Batch Entry clears a pile of receipts in one go.

Budgets that show what's left

Budgets

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is UangRumah really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. UangRumah uses the iCloud account already on your device to keep your iPhone and iPad in sync.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your private iCloud account when you're signed in to iCloud. We don't run any servers, so your data never reaches us.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. Changes sync to your other devices the next time you're online.

Can I share a book with my partner?

Not at the moment. Sync works between devices signed in to the same Apple ID. To share your figures with someone else, send them a PDF report or a CSV export.

Which currencies and languages are supported?

More than 150 currencies, and the full app in English, Bahasa Indonesia and Bahasa Melayu. You can change both anytime in Settings → Preferences. One currency is used across the whole app.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. UangRumah is available for iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16 or later.
